Submission Standards

The must-haves
Don't submit without these

These aren't suggestions. If your submission is missing the basics, it's going to sit in review longer or get passed on entirely

Clear property addressfull address, not just "house in Phoenix." We need to pull comps

Contract or asking pricewhat's the number? We can't underwrite "make me an offer"

Deal structurecash assignment, subject-to, seller finance, novation, etc. We need to know what we're selling

ARV or compsshow us the spread. If there's no margin, there's no deal for our buyers

Photosinterior and exterior. Phone quality is fine. We need to see what we're marketing

Existing mortgage info (for creative deals)balance, rate, monthly payment, lender

Timelinewhen does the contract expire? When does the seller need to close?

What makes deals move fast

Real spreadIf the numbers work for the buyer, the deal moves. Period
Clean photos and detailsDeals with complete info get marketed same-day
Motivated seller with a timelineUrgency sells. If the seller needs to close in 30 days, buyers pay attention
Desirable marketActive investor markets with demand move faster than rural areas with no buyer pool
Clear title situationNo liens, no probate drama, no clouded title. Clean and ready to close
Reasonable assignment feeA $5K fee on a $150K deal moves. A $30K fee on a $150K deal sits

What gets deals rejected

No contractIf you don't have it locked up, we can't market it
Inflated numbersPadding the ARV to make the spread look better doesn't fool our underwriting
No photosBuyers want to see the property. "I'll get photos later" kills momentum
Unrealistic assignment feeIf the fee eats the buyer's profit, nobody bites
Incomplete submissionMissing address, missing price, missing structure. We need the basics to move
Expired or expiring contractIf it closes in 5 days and you're just now submitting, it's too late
